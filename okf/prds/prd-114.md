# PRD-114 (idea): an implicit-solvent AV — RISM PMF × tethered-chain propagator

**Status:** idea, not started
**Done:** Nothing -- research only.
**Missing:** Everything. The formulation is written down; no stage has run.
**Next:** Stage 0 is a stop gate: multiply one AmberTools W(r) into the existing AV/rotamer weights and see whether the PRD-108 AV<->rotamer table moves toward MD/experiment.


## 1. The question, and why the literal version does not exist

Wanted: an accessible volume whose dye density comes out of a mean-field
liquid-state theory — sterics, solvent and dye–surface interactions in one
consistent object — instead of the current split (steric AV in C++;
FRETpredict-style vacuum-LJ rotamer weights ± Debye–Hückel in
`scoring/mean_field.py` / `RotamerEnsemble`; no solvation anywhere).

RISM cannot do this directly. It returns the density g(r) of
**translationally free solvent species** around a fixed solute. A tethered dye
is either part of the solute (then its coordinates are input, not output) or a
free species (then the tether is gone). Polymer RISM (Schweizer–Curro PRISM)
treats chains in bulk, not one grafted chain; 3D-RISM only supports rigid
intramolecular ω for solvent species. **No "RISM of a single tethered chain"
exists in the literature** — for FRET dyes or otherwise.

## 2. The formulation that does work

Factorise the dye density into what RISM is good at and what polymer field
theory is good at:

```
ρ_dye(r) ∝ G_linker(r | anchor) · exp(−β W(r))
```

* **W(r)** — solvent-averaged potential of mean force of the *free*
  chromophore (or a few-site reduced model of it) against the protein in
  water + salt. Obtained by
  * solute–solute RISM at infinite dilution (Mrugalla & Kast 2016; gives
    W and its derivatives w.r.t. dye charge/σ/ε), or
  * 3D-RISM with the chromophore as a **dilute co-solvent species** — the
    "ligand mapping" protocol (Imai/Kovalenko 2009, 3D-RISM-Dock 2012); one
    `rism3d.snglpnt` (AmberTools, multi-species solvent) per structure,
    output on a grid.
* **G_linker(r | anchor)** — end-segment distribution of a flexible chain
  grafted at the anchor atom, in the external field U(r) = W(r) + hard
  obstacles. Polymer SCF/DFT, the Edwards propagator
  `∂q/∂s = (b²/6) ∇²q − βU(r) q`, q(·, 0) = δ(anchor), integrated over
  contour length s = linker length; ρ ∝ q(r, L). Standard theory
  (Fredrickson SCFT; polymer DFT of tethered chains).

Two properties that make this a natural bff extension rather than a new
program:

1. **Today's AV is a limit of it.** W → hard-core and "zero temperature"
   in G (Dijkstra shortest path instead of the entropic propagator) gives
   the steric AV; wAV / dye-weighted AVs are ad-hoc versions of the
   `exp(−βW)` factor. Every existing AV pin remains a limiting-case test.
2. **The propagator is a diffusion equation in contour length** — the same
   discretisation family as `GridDiffusionSolver` (Smoluchowski form,
   PRD-109) on the PRD-105 lattice. Grid, obstacle raster and windowing
   already exist.

## 3. Kast's work that is the theoretical basis (literature note)

* Schmidt & Kast, *J. Phys. Chem. B* 106, 6289 (2002); Kast,
  *ChemPhysChem* 5, 449 (2004) — **hybrid integral equation / MC**: sample
  solute conformations, RISM supplies Δμ_solv per conformer. The discrete
  (rotamer-library) counterpart of §2: `q_i ∝ exp(−β[E_intra + E_pair + Δμ_i])`.
* Kast, Hauptmann, Schilling, *Chem. Phys. Lett.* 418, 202 (2006) —
  **reduced molecular models by inverting RISM**: solvent-averaged effective
  potentials for a coarse-grained model with fewer sites. How to collapse
  chromophore + linker into few sites (for W(r), for the CG dye beads in
  `cgdye`, for the effective segment length b).
* Mrugalla & Kast, *J. Phys.: Condens. Matter* 28, 344004 (2016) —
  **solute–solute RISM PMFs and their analytical parameter derivatives**;
  supplies W(r) and "how sticky is this dye and why".
* Güssregen, Matter, Hessler, Lionta, Heil, Kast, *JCIM* 57, 1652 (2017) —
  3D-RISM **free-energy densities / hydration sites**; per-voxel ΔG
  decomposition (diagnostic: why a patch is favourable).
* Kloss & Kast, *JCP* 128, 134505 (2008); Heil & Kast, *JCP* 142, 114107
  (2015) — charged solutes / electrolytes / fast electrostatics in 3D-RISM;
  needed for charged dyes near nucleic acids (salt enters W(r) instead of DH).
* Sharma … Kast … Marx, *JCTC* 17, 6366 (2021); Tran … Kast … Neese,
  *JPCA* 127, 6447 (2023) — EC-RISM for **nitroxide spin probes in water**:
  the group has already treated a tethered, environment-sensitive probe.
  Nearest existing analogue; Kast is at TU Dortmund (PC III) — collaboration
  hook.
* Kast, *PCCP* 3, 5087 (2001); *Phys. Rev. E* 67, 041203 (2003) — hybrid
  simulation/IE corrections, path-independent free energies (background).
* Non-Kast recipe for W(r): Imai, Kovalenko et al., *JACS* 131, 12430
  (2009) ligand mapping; 3D-RISM-Dock (2012); Kovalenko–Hirata binding-site
  protocol, *JCTC* 2020. Tethered-chain field theory: reviews of DFT of
  tethered chains (Borówko/Sokołowski), Fredrickson SCFT; "Kinetics of
  tethered ligands binding to a surface receptor", *Macromolecules* 50, 8810
  (2017) is a single grafted ligand density near a surface.

## 4. Caveats known up front

* KH/HNC closures **over-bind hydrophobic contacts**; W(r) is
  semi-quantitative and will need a scaling knob (as σ/ε scaling does today),
  validated against the MD-derived AVs already in `okf/validation/`.
* Chromophore is anisotropic; point-dye AV is a rigid-body-with-orientation
  problem. Options: 2–4 site reduced model (Kast 2006) or orientational
  average of W.
* Ideal-chain propagator has no self-avoidance — same assumption as the AV;
  fine for 10–20 Å linkers.
* Grids compatible (RISM 0.3–0.5 Å, AV 0.5–1 Å). One W(r) per structure is
  the expensive part (minutes, CPU); the propagator per labelling site is
  cheap and would sit where the Dijkstra reach sits now.

## 5. If it is ever picked up — candidate stages, with a stop gate

* **Stage 0 (gate, no solver code)** — take one W(r) from AmberTools
  3D-RISM for a well-characterised case (T4L or hGBP1 sites of PRD-108's
  AV↔rotamer table, Alexa488 chromophore as co-solvent species) and simply
  multiply it into the existing AV / rotamer weights. Compare against the
  MD-derived dye densities and the experimental Σχ² of
  `okf/validation/av_vs_rotamer.md`. **If exp(−βW) does not move the table
  toward MD/experiment, stop here** — the propagator refinement cannot rescue
  a wrong field.
* **Stage 1** — Edwards propagator on the PRD-105 lattice (contour-length
  diffusion, hard obstacles from `AVOccupancyMap`, anchor δ-source); pin the
  W=hard-core limit against the Dijkstra AV; entropic weighting vs wAV.
* **Stage 2** — W(r) with electrolyte for nucleic-acid sites; charged dyes;
  compare with the DH option of `RotamerEnsemble`.
* **Stage 3** — reduced-site chromophore model (Kast 2006) and orientation
  (κ² needs the dipole, not just the centre).

Where it would live: `representation/` (it is a representation of the dye
density, like AV and RotamerEnsemble), field solver reused from `dynamics/`
grid code; W(r) is an external input file (grid) at first, never a RISM solver
inside bff.
