# Quenching anisotropy from the rotamers

**Status:** proposed, plan only
**Done:** Nothing -- the decision gates are written down.
**Missing:** Everything; no implementation.
**Next:** The first gate: kill-test the continuum model before building the exchange-coupled rotamer master equation.


## Why

The implemented anisotropy model (`qpinn/forward.py::simulate`) is a
**bound/free population mixture bolted onto a continuum point-dye**. The
rotamer library enters only as measured scalars (contact probability, sticky
shell, S^2 diagnostics); the per-rotamer geometry is never load-bearing. Each
physical effect had to be added as a channel — sticking `theta(r)`, then the
static bound state (2026-08-22, five channels) — and the anisotropy ranking
still does not move in the forward model (s21 negative, s12 magnitude-only;
only the joint-field reduction `<contact*kq>` ranks, +0.639).

Three structural facts say this is the wrong object, not a missing parameter:

1. **The anisotropy correlation is nonlocal in position space.** What governs
   `r_inf` is `<P2(mu_i . mu_j)>` between *specific* rotamers — a rank-2
   correlation on conformer *pairs*. No per-voxel tensor field `Q(r)` can
   represent it (s11's ceiling: `r0*S2 = 0.064` vs measured 0.196, 12x low).
2. **A dye whose linker is frozen cannot have its anisotropy changed by
   quenching at all** — each molecule keeps `r(t) = r0` until the protein
   rotates. Quenching-anisotropy coupling exists *only through conformational
   exchange coupled to geometry*: fast-exchanging contact rotamers are removed
   before they decorrelate, leaving a rigid, short-lifetime, high-r species.
   A mixture model fakes this with channels; a rotamer model *is* it.
3. **The 2026-08-22 static-bound correction becomes a theorem.** A bound dye
   that still emits with its own (shorter) lifetime and rotates with the
   protein is exactly "contact rotamers with large k_i dying before they
   decorrelate" — no separate channel, no `w_static` knob.

And the empirical edge: the fitted 17-parameter model reaches `tau_x`
rho = +0.573 against the parameter-free AV-exposure baseline's +0.571 (s31
p200) — **17 fitted parameters add approximately nothing** — while 16 of 41
sites sat below the max-quench floor until the static channel was added
(s33b/s33d). The continuum may be adequate or it may be at its limit; nobody
has asked per-site.

**So the plan is decision-gated, cheapest first.** Whether the continuum can
work is question zero; the rotamer model is built only if it cannot.

## The object (stages 1+)

Per site, the state space is the **N dye rotamers** the stack already ships
(`RotamerEnsemble`: per-rotamer `weights`, `mu`, full `atoms`; N ~ 711 at
cutoff10). Exchange generator **L** (N x N Markov generator, from MD — stage
1), death rates **k_i** from real per-rotamer atom geometry. With
`M = L - diag(k)`:

```
F(t) = sum_i  w_i (e^{Mt} 1)_i                                # intensity decay
A(t) = e^{-t/rho_prot} sum_ij w_i [e^{Mt}]_ij P2(mu_i . mu_j) # anisotropy numerator
r(t) = r0 * A(t) / F(t)
```

What this subsumes with no extra channels: the static bound state (still
emits, short tau, r ~ r0); `r_inf/r0 = <P2>` of the slowest eigenmode of M
("bound fraction" and "order parameter" are both special cases — the
operational definition s12 arrived at, now derived); the 4.5x orientational
average gap (`Dye.effective_reach`) — k_i is evaluated per rotamer with real
atom positions, then Boltzmann-averaged, so the field model's orientational
average error vanishes; and the machinery survives verbatim — `eigen.py`'s
spectral view applies to M directly, the surrogate's per-mode
`(lambda_i, cF_i, phi_i)` architecture is unchanged, VV/VH training and
`reduce_curves` are reused. Only the generator changes: 40^3 lattice -> N x N.

## Stages

### Stage 0 — `s39_continuum_verdict.py`: can the continuum reach the data?

~1 day, no new physics. Two tests:

- **Per-site reachability.** Fit the 17 parameters *per site* (deliberate
  overfit) using the fast surrogate — after first checking that the
  surrogate's held-out error (0.057 ns) is not the limiter. A site no
  parameter vector reaches within measurement error is **continuum-dead**.
- **Floors.** Extend s33b/s33d: max-quench floor + static floor per site;
  a site below both is dead regardless of parameters.

**Gate rule (hard).** If >= ~85-90 % of the 41 sites are reachable AND the
global fit beats the s30 baseline (`tau_x` rho +0.571) by a real margin, the
continuum is adequate: **the PRD closes with that finding and stages 1+ are
cancelled.** Otherwise proceed — and the dead sites, with their structural
signature (buried? high `r_inf`? short `tau_x`?), become the rotamer model's
primary targets and its headline metric.

Honest framing: current evidence says *marginal* (+0.573 vs +0.571). Stage 0
makes that verdict per-site and precise instead of one pooled correlation.

### Stage 1 — `s40_exchange_msm.py`: measure L from the shipped MD

Assign the 28,110 A48_C1R trajectory frames to rotamer states (the 711
cutoff10 conformers as centroids, `rms_no_align` — both shipped), count
transitions at lag tau, fit a **reversible MSM -> generator L**. 

**Gate (hard):** the model `C_int(t) = sum_ij w_i [e^{Lt}]_ij P2(mu_i.mu_j)`
must reproduce the trajectory's *direct* dipole autocorrelation
`<P2(mu(0).mu(t))>`; report implied `rho_conf` and effective state count.
Else STOP.

Caveat stated in the output: the trajectory is the free (capped) dye — L
carries linker chemistry, not surface slowing. Site screening reweights `w`;
the surface slowdown enters at stage 3 as the L-scaling exponent. Fallbacks if
the MSM fails: strong-mixing single `rho_conf` (1a), Metropolis on rotamer
energies via shipped `rotamer_interaction_energies` (1b).

### Stage 2 — `s41_rotamer_master.py`: the forward model

Per site: `load_ensemble` -> per-rotamer `k_i = 1/tau0 + kQ_aa *
exp(-(d_i - d0)/rc) * g(chi_i)` with **real conformer-atom distances** to
`QUENCHER_ATOMS` moiety centroids (not one distance from a dipole midpoint)
and ring-ring angle chi_i (Vaiana stacking geometry); then F(t), A(t) from
`M = L - diag(k)`, differentiable via `torch.linalg.matrix_exp` (N ~ 711,
trivial). VV/VH conversion and `reduce_curves` reused unchanged.

**Gates (hard, kill the stage if any fails):**
- frozen limit (L = 0): `r(t) = r0 e^{-t/rho_prot}` exactly;
- strong-mixing limit: reduces to the Lipari-Szabo closed form;
- a static-bound species (short-tau, r ~ r0) **emerges** with no separate
  channel when contact rotamers carry large k_i;
- `r_inf/r0` = `<P2>` of the slowest mode of M, and matches the 2-component
  finite-window fit applied to the model curves (eigen.py's warning about
  asymptotic vs finite-window `r_inf`).

### Stage 3 — `s42_fit_pet.py`: global fit + site-specific L scaling

Fit the PET table (+ d0, rc, g parameters) on all 41 sites jointly
(multi-site mandatory — PRD-110/111), **plus one global exponent alpha**
scaling the exchange *off-rates* of contact rotamers by
`slow_factor^{n_contact(i)}` — the continuum's `D(r)` slowdown transplanted
onto the rotamer graph, so L is not purely protein-free.

**Gate (hard):** beat both the stage-0 continuum fit and the s30 baseline on
`tau_x`; headline metric = the continuum-dead sites closing. Report Fisher
identifiability — PRD-110's 1.4e10 condition number should collapse, since
the drift/sticking/`w_static` knobs no longer exist. Else STOP.

### Stage 4 — the learned components: backprop to the quenching and to the interaction potential

Gated on stage 3 (the physics-only fit must first be characterised — the
learned terms are there to close what it leaves open, and their value is
measured against it). The master equation is differentiable end-to-end, and
the two things worth learning are exactly the two fields the hand-written
model carries as fitted constants:

- **`s43_learned_kdchi.py` — backprop to the quenching.**
  `k_i = 1/tau0 + f_theta(d_i, chi_i, aa)` with a small MLP (amino-acid
  embedding), constrained `k >= 1/tau0` and monotone-decreasing in d,
  trained across all sites through `e^{Mt}`. **Payoff check:** does the
  learned `g(chi)` rediscover the Vaiana pi-stacking criterion (~5 A
  ring-centre distance, ~30 deg plane-normal angle)? If yes, the model has
  learned the stacking geometry nobody coded.
- **`s43b_learned_potential.py` — backprop to the interaction potential.**
  The rotamer weights are Boltzmann over the screening energy,
  `w_i prop exp(-(E_steric + E_elec + dU_theta(x_i))/kT)`; learn a
  correction `dU_theta` on per-rotamer site features `x_i` (contacts by
  amino acid, charge adjacency, exposure) — differentiable through the
  weights into F and A. This is the *identifiable* way to reshape `w_i`
  (a global theta shared across sites, where per-site `w_i` is amplitude
  degeneracy), and it subsumes the **per-amino-acid sticking `s_a` gap** in
  `model-terms.md`. **Payoff check:** does the learned stickiness
  localise to Lys/Arg, as Prakash's localisation and `x_trapped,dark`
  measured? Both learned terms are regularised toward zero so the data must
  pay for every learned structure, and the Fisher is reported — a learned
  term that recreates PRD-110's blind directions is deleted, not shipped.

### Stage 5 — `s44_surrogate_regen.py`: surrogate on rotamer spectra (conditional)

Only if the rotamer model survives. Regenerate the s23 pretrain dataset from
rotamer-generator spectra; retrain s24/s29/s31 unchanged. Gate: held-out
error <= current 0.057 ns / 0.021.

## The differentiability ladder — what "backprop to the rotamer" means here

The master equation is differentiable end-to-end and everything after the
geometry is smooth and small, so gradients reach the rotamers at three rungs:

- **Level 1 — to the interaction potential (the weights).** `w_i` enters F, A
  linearly (amplitudes), but per-site `w_i` is unidentifiable amplitude
  degeneracy — PRD-110's stopped gate is the precedent. The identifiable
  object one level up is the **interaction potential** the weights are
  Boltzmann of: a learned correction `dU_theta` on site features (stage 4,
  `s43b`), or an effective temperature / energy scale multiplying the
  screening Boltzmann weights (realised inside stage 3).
- **Level 2 — to the quenching rates.** Stage 4, `s43`: per-rotamer features
  `(d_i, chi_i, aa)` -> MLP -> `k_i`, backprop through `e^{Mt}` to theta.
- **Level 3 — to the geometry.** With a torch port of the `.drot`
  `internal2cart` (`prototypes/drot_rotlib/rotlib.py`), each rotamer's
  `d_i, chi_i` are differentiable in its **own dihedrals**, so an observable
  loss can pull a learned PMF `U(chi)` over the linker's torsion space — the
  observables reshape the rotamers, a potential *inside* the dye rather than
  between dye and site. Likewise `L = L_MD + (symmetric low-rank correction)`,
  detailed balance preserved by construction (the same symmetrisation
  `eigen.py` uses). **Conditional extension** of `s43b` — taken only if the
  site-feature potential leaves a named residual the feature level cannot
  express.

Honest naming: nothing replaces the master equation; the networks live
*inside* its terms — a universal differential equation in PRD-115's sense,
**not** a collocation PINN (PRD-110's stage 0 already stopped that). In the
prototype this is torch (`prototypes/` is exempt); a C++ landing later is the
sparse generator + `MlpCore.h`, PRD-115-style.

## Relationship to the continuum twin

The continuum twin is the **defendant in stage 0, not a permanent
compartment**. If it passes, it stays and everything from stage 1 on is
cancelled. If it fails, the hybrid free-diffusion fallback is dropped too —
pure rotamer — because the physics lives at contact scale (s12: magnitude
closes at the 8-10 A shell = rotamer scale). s11/s12/s33d are superseded for
anisotropy if the rotamer path proceeds, and kept as history either way.

## Risks

- **L transferability**: only A48_C1R has raw MD; the other 33 libraries are
  clustered only. Fallbacks 1a/1b; level-3 learned L-correction is the
  eventual answer.
- **L is protein-free**: the free-dye trajectory knows linker chemistry, not
  the surface — hence the stage-3 alpha scaling, and the honest reading of
  its fitted value.
- **One ensemble, two sources**: `w` from screened library weights, L from
  the trajectory's own dynamics — consistency between the two must be
  checked (stage 1 reports it).
- **Single `mu` per dye** (no `mu_abs`/`mu_em` split in `libraries.json`) —
  acceptable for rhodamines; flagged for coumarins/cyanines.
- **Quencher side chains frozen at moiety centroids** in v1; Dunbrack
  rotamers via PRD-118 are the later refinement.
- **`r_inf` is the noisy head** (disputed T4L 2-exp vs 3-exp fits; estimated
  `rho_protein` on 3 of 5 proteins) — gate on `tau_x`, report `r_inf`.
