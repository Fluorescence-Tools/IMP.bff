# av.pinn — a general forward PINN that replaces the quenching solver, and works in reverse

**Status:** active (2026-09-06). Forward acceptance met on the frozen 300k checkpoint; reverse acceptance at 88 % coverage; measured-protein result honest but under-determined.
**Done:** §1–§7 of the plan: physics fix, symmetry assemblies, spectrum head + exact decode, linear-space NLL with the surrogate's own covariance, streaming generator (natural-frequency decoys, Sobol sweeps, restart-safe), voxel-encoder trainer (structure-hashed validation, sliding window), forward and reverse acceptance scripts, identifiability-vs-sites study, cross-validated fit against measured sites.
**Missing:** r_inf clears its bar only on the frozen milestone (0.0199 vs 0.02) and straddles it between checkpoints — a loss-design floor, not data (five interventions tried, all null). The measured-protein out-of-sample result (+0.35) is limited by 23 sites, not by the surrogate.
**Next:** more measured sites (the identifiability table says what kind); steady-state anisotropy as a third functional (74 Alexa488 sites in the bundle); the 1M decision only if the r_inf curve moves.

## Why

Everything before this PRD was a **fit against 23 measured sites** with a surrogate as a means. That is the wrong product. The surrogate *is* the product: a network general enough that any experiment can later be optimised against it — quenching constants, sticking per amino-acid class, static binding, dye/linker geometry refined from measured observables. The 23-site fit is one consumer.

What the descriptor line had established, and this plan rests on (all in `prototypes/quench_pinn/`, figures s48–s84):

- the hand-built 20-number site descriptor was the binding constraint: a 3D encoder over the voxelised site generalises **1.64× better on τ_x** at 105k samples and the gap *widens* with data (s60, s76); capacity does not help (s63, s74);
- the static/dark channel had **never existed in any dataset** (`w_static = 0`), and the first dataset that switched it on was 70 % fully dark because the binding energy was a sum of five terms each up to 15 kT;
- `eigen.py` lacks that channel entirely and is not the supervision source — the solver is;
- reading r_inf off a latent mode was 91× worse than reducing it from the curves (s54): **physical quantities are functionals through the forward map, never read-offs from latent coordinates.**

## Already exists (close nothing)

- `qpinn/forward.py` — the solver `FW.simulate`, `reduce_curves` (τ_x by trapezoid; r_inf by a 2×2 weighted least squares), `build_fields`. Truth for everything here.
- `qpinn/sites.py`, `qpinn/assembly.py`, `qpinn/voxel.py` — site geometry, accessible volume via LabelLib, symmetry-operator assemblies, the 16-channel lattice.
- `qpinn/measurements.py` — the loader over `prototypes/fast_label_score/okf/data/pinn_table.csv`, with the ingestion gates of that bundle (`INGESTION.md`).
- `s29_all_proteins.py` — the descriptor-line experimental fit; its `per_site`, `QPINN_HOLDOUT`, `cv_folds*.json` and pooling readers were reused, not rebuilt.
- PRD-110 (stopped: θ not identifiable from one decay) and PRD-111 (identifiable *across* sites, joint Fisher information): this PRD is the operational consequence of PRD-111.

## The decisions (tpeulen, 2026-09-02, by interview)

1. **Product.** `(structure, physical parameters) → NN → VV(t), VH(t)`; projection to τ_x, r_inf, FRET exact and downstream; differentiable, usable in reverse.
2. **Output form.** Signed amplitudes on a fixed log rate grid (K = 64, 0.02–200 /ns), channels {para, perp}, decoded by one exact matrix. Abstract — not `eigen.py`, not ucfret's grid.
3. **Loss.** Gaussian NLL of the solver's VV/VH under the head's Gaussian pushed through the linear decode; the covariance is the surrogate's own error and reaches the reverse fit.
4. **Input.** 3D voxel encoder + every physical parameter incl. the AV parameters varied per site.
5. **Physics fix first.** One binding energy scaled by amino-acid class, `E_tot·s_c·c(r)`, classes partitioning every residue (AROM, CAT, ANI, ALIPH, OTHER); same for the transient term.
6. **Data.** Continuous streaming from the 51k-entry filtered PDB pool, ~10 % of structures held out whole plus the measured proteins as a fixed test set; Sobol sweep ~32 draws × 4 AV settings per site; decoys on; milestones 100k / 300k / 1M.
7. **Acceptance — forward.** On held-out structures: τ_x < 0.12 ns, r_inf < 0.02 (the measurement σ), calibrated (z-sd 0.8–1.25, coverage 0.90–0.98).
8. **Acceptance — reverse.** Solver experiments at known θ on held-out structures, noise at the real σ, θ recovered through the PINN with its covariance in the likelihood; truth inside the posterior at stated coverage, with an explicit list of what the data cannot determine.
9. **Decoys follow the natural surface amino-acid distribution** (2026-09-03), measured on 400 pool structures (`s85`, `qpinn/aa_frequencies.json`).
10. **AV parameters are not fitted in reverse** (2026-09-05): the voxel lattice *is* the accessible volume; varying them as inputs against a frozen lattice is off-manifold.
11. **Observables stay τ_x and r_inf** (2026-09-05): the literature being mined carries lifetimes, some anisotropies, essentially no decays.

## Stages and gates

### Stage 1 — physics, assemblies, spectrum head (`qpinn/forward.py`, `assembly.py`, `spectrum.py`) — done 2026-09-02
Gate: dark fraction < 25 % (was 70 %; now 10 %); assembly rejection 12 % → 0 %; grid round-trip < 1 % in log F. **Finding:** a head emitting `z` in the decode's singular basis did not train (singular values span 15 decades); it emits `w = S z`, the curve's coefficients on the singular directions, with precisions in those units.

### Stage 2 — generator and trainer (`s80_generate_pinn.py`, `s81_train_pinn.py`) — done, running
Gate: two generators at 100 % GPU with < 1 % starvation. Solver-bound at ~3,800 unique samples/h. **Findings that changed the design:** the trainer's validation split was re-drawn on every chunk reload and leaked (now a structure-id hash with pre-fix structures excluded); at 41k samples the net had τ_x 0.029 ns on trained structures and 0.257 ns on held-out ones — the whole error is structure generalisation, so rotation augmentation of the lattice was added; the trainer loading every chunk was OOM-killed silently at 162 chunks / 44 GB (now a sliding window of the newest 128 chunks, milestones counting cumulative samples).

### Stage 3 — the covariance is real (`spectrum.Head`, calibration) — done 2026-09-03/05
The head's log-precision sat *at* its bound for every direction (96 %), so the emitted variance was a constant and z-sd fell as the mean improved. Bound widened and stored in the checkpoint; then two calibration factors measured on held-out structures — curve-level and per functional (τ_x, r_inf) — because the head is 3–4× over-confident on the lifetime and slightly under-confident on the anisotropy, which no single number serves. `tau_var` gained its missing ratio term (2 %, verified by Monte Carlo); `r_inf_var` added analytically through the 2×2 reduction (first-order, 1.1–2× high where the two rotational components go collinear; calibrated). Gate met: z-sd 0.99–1.12, coverage 0.92–0.94 on the fixed test set.

### Stage 4 — forward acceptance (`s82_accept_forward.py`, `s86_learning_curve.py`) — met on the frozen 300k checkpoint, 2026-09-06
| criterion | `pinn_300k.pt` | bar |
|---|---|---|
| τ_x | **0.102 ns** | 0.12 |
| r_inf | **0.0199** | 0.02 |
| z-sd | 0.99 | 0.8–1.25 |
| coverage | 0.94 | 0.90–0.98 |

Learning-curve slopes 0.45 (τ_x) and 0.25 (r_inf) against unique samples. **r_inf straddles its bar between checkpoints** (0.019–0.024; the test-set standard error is 0.0002, so this is SGD wander, not noise). Diagnosed (`s92`): the mean is set by the quarter of sites whose two rotational decays are nearly collinear (error 0.0095 → 0.0435 across cond(A) quartiles; median 0.010; 73 % of samples individually under the bar); the target itself is stable; train error equals test error for r_inf (no generalisation gap) while τ_x generalises normally — **a loss/representation floor, not data.** Five interventions in one day, all null or harmful: lower variance floor, wider precision bound with reset (the head re-saturates; a heteroscedastic NLL gives hard directions *more* variance, i.e. less weight), log-space NLL (diverges from this start: τ_x 0.115 → 0.139, r_inf 0.068), an explicit relative term on VV−VH at the measured equal-gradient scale (null at 30 % and 100 %; destructive at 13×). Accepted as is.

### Stage 5 — reverse acceptance (`s83_accept_reverse.py`) — passes, 2026-09-05/06
The chain of controls that found the defect: exact-model control (PINN generates the data) still gave 0/31 coverage → not surrogate bias; no common mode across sites; 24 → 48 sites did not move the error → not data volume; **the raw autograd Hessian is non-finite** and its pseudo-inverse gave zero width for every parameter, which forces "identified, not covered" mechanically. Posterior now from the Gauss-Newton information (first derivatives only, PSD by construction); convergence judged by the Newton decrement `gᵀH⁻¹g ≤ 0.01·NF` and `max|step|/sd ≤ 0.1`, not a gradient norm — the L-BFGS stall (8 iterations / 40 evaluations, gradient reproducible to 0.0) was a kink from `maximum(pushed variance, floor²)`, removed. Gate: exact-model coverage 27/28, 26/28 (95 %); solver data **26/28, 23/28 (88 %)** once the surrogate error at θ* fell to 0.105–0.14 ns; seven parameters genuinely pinned by 24 sites.

### Stage 6 — identifiability versus sites (`s90_identifiability_vs_sites.py`) — done 2026-09-06
Nested site sets, three truth draws, solver and exact-model arms, on a 131-structure held-out set (the first run on 35 structures had coverage falling with site count because sites shared structures). Sites for a parameter's posterior to fall below half its prior, both observables per site: `rc` 2, `E_stat` 14, `kq_HIS` 14, `qy_bound` 17, `s_stat_CAT` 20, `s_stat_ALIPH` 27, `r0` 31, `tau0` 33, `kq_PRO` 34, `kq_MET` 40, `kq_CYS` 41, **`kq_TRP` 43**, `d_free` 48, `rho_protein` 53, `s_stat_OTHER` 56, `kq_TYR` 56, `w_stick` 69, `rho_dye` 74, `s_stat_AROM` 80, `s_stat_ANI` 86; `q_dye` 201, `slow_bound` 209, `debye` 346, the five dynamic sticking scales 200–370. **Lifetimes only:** the quenching constants barely change (`kq_TRP` 33, `kq_TYR` 42); the anisotropy physics is τ-blind (`r0` 373, `rho_dye` 293, `rho_protein` 208, `w_stick` 308). Deliverable for the literature search: ~45 sites with both observables for the chemistry, ~90 for the rest; one anisotropy-carrying site is worth 5–10 lifetime-only sites for the rotational and sticking parameters.

### Stage 7 — the measured proteins, honestly (`s88_measured_pinn.py`) — done 2026-09-06
Per SITE (119 records collapse to 33 sites; one T4L site appeared 21 times and every earlier in-sample number had counted it 21×), shared physics fitted on T4L + hGBP1, PSD-95 and LiF predicted only (AlphaFold models, excluded by an earlier decision). Through-origin slopes after refinement 1.017 ± 0.029 (τ_x) and 1.019 ± 0.047 (r_inf): the model reproduces the measured *scale* on both channels. **Leave-one-site-out, 23 folds:** τ_x r +0.28 / ρ +0.35 / RMSE 0.48 ns (in-sample +0.60 / +0.62 / 0.35); r_inf r +0.09 (in-sample +0.55). Against the descriptor line's held-out ρ +0.42 the two are indistinguishable at 23 sites (95 % CI ±0.22). The surrogate's own error on these sites is 0.11 ns; the residual is the physics model against the measurements, under-determined at 23 sites exactly as Stage 6 predicts.

## Checks (the ledger)

- Every result is a PNG with a `.txt` caption in `prototypes/quench_pinn/figures/` (s80–s93), pulled with `pull_from_cordeshub.sh`.
- Acceptance loops score the fixed 920-geometry test set every 4 h (`accept_forward_loop.log`); the reverse test re-armed itself when the τ_x bar was crossed.
- Checkpoints record the head's bound and all three calibration factors and are written atomically; scoring scripts read those from the checkpoint, never from the code.

## Risks

- **r_inf at its bar without margin.** Accepting it means reporting r_inf with mean, median and the cond(A) breakdown, never as a hard floor on what the model knows.
- **Surrogate bias in the reverse fit** shows as the posterior narrows (solver-arm coverage 0.86 → 0.71 with site count in Stage 6). More training or an ensemble covariance is the remedy; neither is scheduled.
- **The sliding window** stops replaying data older than ~35 h; if r_inf drifts up over days, that is the first suspect.
- **The bundle**: for the four proteins fitted, not one of 238 values carries a reported measurement error; σ = 0.12 ns / 0.02 is assumed and stated as such on every figure.

## Non-goals (decided)

Replacing the AV computation with the NN. Matching ucfret's grid or GSVD basis. FRET channels (the channel axis is extensible). Physical-variation covariance. Fitting the AV parameters in reverse (needs an outer loop that rebuilds the lattice). Decay-curve observables (the literature does not carry them).
