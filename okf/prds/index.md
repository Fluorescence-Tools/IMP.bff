## bff-local PRDs

* 📐 [PRD-116: extending the dye rotamer library — generating an entry, and the parity bar that says it is one](prd-116.md) — **status: proposed 2026-08-19, nothing started.** Six dyes are wanted (Cy3B, Cy3, sulfoCy5, ATTO 488, ATTO 647N, Alexa 647); three ship. The check that established that found three defects: `Cy3_C2R` and `Cy5_C2R` are **declared in `libraries.json` with no data behind them**, so asking for them fails inside the loader rather than reporting an unavailable library; `C3N_C2R` ships complete and **undeclared**, unreachable by name; and `Clusters_populations.csv` disagrees with the weights it describes on **56 of 93 rows**, in both directions, while nothing in the stack reads it. Underneath is the bigger fact: **no library in this repository has ever been generated** — every entry is FRETpredict's (Montepietra 2024, GPLv3), and the FRETpredict parity pins are pinned *to them*, so a generated entry is a different object wearing the same file layout and must say so in its registry metadata. The sampling (`LangevinDyeSampler`, `generate_linker_rotamers`, `cluster_frames_leader`) and the file writing (`bin/imp_bff_traj2bcif`) both already exist; what does not is anything that produces the four-part entry. The gate is **regeneration, not plausibility**: reproduce `A48_C1R` — the entry the pins already cover — on ⟨κ²⟩, R_DA and the E_static/E_dyn1/E_dyn2 triple at a tolerance stated in advance, before generating anything new. **Blocked on** [`impropers_are_dropped.md`](../validation/impropers_are_dropped.md): sampling a dye whose ring feature is `rb NO, md_fixed NO` under a force field with no improper terms would bake a pyramidalising chromophore into shipped data, and the transition dipole is defined by that ring. Stage 1 (make the registry honest) is small, independent of the block, and lands on its own.
* 📐 [PRD-115: a differentiable lattice — adjoint of the field solver, per-voxel features, and a learned field on `MlpCore.h`](prd-115.md) — **status: proposed 2026-08-19, nothing coded on the bff side.** Built on two PRD-111 findings: `slow_factor`/`contact_distance` are one parameter (the data determine the mobility *field*, so re-parameterise on a smooth function of per-voxel features), and every gradient in the stack is a finite difference through a 1–10 s solve. Plan: (0) hand adjoint of `diffusion_propagate` — the sweep is linear in `cur`, so the reverse pass is the transposed 7-point stencil (Smoluchowski self-adjoint up to the mask, Itô not) with √n checkpointing; gate is the dot-product identity vs the forward solver; (1) `voxel_features` kernel over reachable voxels only; (2) a learned `D(r)`/`k(r)` on the vendored `MlpCore.h` (tttrlib T-20260819-01) fitted to the six PRD-111 sites with fewer effective parameters; (3, optional) drift term for a learned potential of mean force (PRD-114's `W(r)`). Not a PINN — nothing replaces the solver; no torch/jax; rotational dynamics named as out of scope (no orientational DOF exists). Also lists what tttrlib already covers; the two gaps found (scaler and JSON loader not in `MlpCore.h`) were closed the same day (tttrlib `8ac9b0a52`), and a bff test compiles against bff's own headers and evaluates/differentiates a tttrlib-trained model to 1e-12.
* 💡 [PRD-114 (idea): an implicit-solvent AV — RISM potential of mean force × tethered-chain propagator](prd-114.md) — **status: idea, research only (2026-08-19), not started.** Today's AV is purely steric and the rotamer weights are vacuum LJ ± Debye–Hückel; nothing knows about water. Literal "RISM instead of AV" does not exist (RISM gives densities of free species; a tethered dye is neither free nor a solvent). The formulation that works: ρ_dye(r) ∝ G_linker(r|anchor)·exp(−βW(r)) — W(r) the solvent-averaged PMF of the free chromophore vs protein from RISM (Kast solute–solute RISM / 3D-RISM co-solvent "ligand mapping"), G_linker the Edwards propagator of the tether on the AV grid (a diffusion equation in contour length — the family bff already has). Steric AV is its hard-core, zero-entropy limit. Kast literature note (hybrid IE/MC 2002/2004, reduced models 2006, solute–solute PMFs 2016, free-energy densities 2017, nitroxide probes 2021/23) and a stage plan whose stage 0 is a stop gate: multiply one AmberTools W(r) into the existing AV/rotamer weights and see whether the PRD-108 AV↔rotamer table moves toward MD/experiment.

* 🔨 [PRD-113: `IMP.bff` as a forward-model engine — restructure around Dye, Label, Representation, Dynamics, Photophysics](prd-113.md) — **status: in progress.** `IMP.bff` is to structure what `tttrlib` is to photons: it emits **experiment-neutral** quantities (lifetime spectra, rate constants, κ² distributions, distances) and leaves convolution, IRF, pileup and fitting outside. The tree said otherwise — three unreconciled migrations left a whole TCSPC instrument layer with a single consumer, two AV builders over one C++ core, three distance layers, two κ² modules, two descriptions of a dye, `RotamerEnsemble(AccessibleVolume)` inheriting from the concrete AV class, and 42 numba kernels behind a shim that silently substitutes slow Python. Restructures around the standard MD decomposition — species / system / representation / integrator / interaction terms / observables — with **all numerics in C++** (numpy and numba become prototyping tools), a **domain-scoped** public surface, and layout `dye/ label/ representation/ dynamics/ photophysics/ observables/ restraints/ io/`. Executed as in-place vertical slices, port-per-slice, because a flat C++ namespace cannot hold two `average_distance` — the port is what forces the de-duplication.

* ✅ [PRD-111: what has to be measured — the quenching parameters are identifiable across sites, not within one](prd-111.md) — **status: stages 0 and 1 measured, both gates pass.** PRD-110 stopped because θ is not recoverable from one decay. θ is *global* (one dye, one chemistry, one mobility) while the geometry is *per site*, so N decays share one θ and the joint Fisher information is the **sum** — which beats its terms only if the blind directions rotate with geometry. Six T4L chain-A sites spanning quencher-in-contact (A124, TRP126 at 3.5 Å) to nearly-bare (A53): joint condition number **1.8 × 10⁵** against 1.96 × 10⁸ for the best single site and 1.43 × 10¹⁰ for A132. The rotation is the whole effect — joint `λ_min` 152.7 against 0.0456 for the sum of the per-site minima, a **3 350× gain**; second principal angles reach 88.4°. A joint fit confirms it: `free_diffusion` −52 % → **+2.5 %**, `kQ_scale` −69 % → **+4.3 %**, and in **97 evaluations against 676**. Two qualifications, both actionable: **`contact_distance` and `slow_factor` are one parameter, not two** — the fit reaches **reduced χ² = 1.016** at a `contact_distance` 33 % away from truth, reproducibly across resolutions, because `slow_factor^n(contact_distance)` builds the *same* mobility field (log D correlation 0.927, equilibrium overlap **98.7 %**); no number of sites separates them, `JᵀJ` error bars must not be quoted for either (the Fisher is confidently wrong about its *best*-determined parameter), and the model needs re-parameterising rather than more data. And the stated hypothesis was **wrong** — diffusion sensitivity tracks *quenched fraction*, not distance to quencher (corr −0.56): transport is rate-limiting when quenching depletes, so **to measure mobility, label where the dye is strongly quenched.** Repeated end to end at 2.0 Å with the same conclusion, so not a grid artefact. **Stage 1** perturbs the model two ways. A per-site free-dye fraction of 2–7 %, *unmodelled*, wipes out the gain (`free_diffusion` −60.5 %, `kQ_scale` −29.5 %) — but at reduced χ² 1.683, a **9.4 σ** rejection, so it announces itself; modelled (11 parameters) it restores χ² to 1.021 and brings `kQ_scale`/`rC`/`slow_factor` back to a few percent, with `free_diffusion` paying the cost at −13 % (a free fraction and fast diffusion both raise the long-time intensity). A **5 % linker-length error is benign** — `kQ_scale` −1.6 %, χ² 1.110 (1.5 σ) — the opposite of the expected answer: **worry about the sample, not the structure.** All data still synthetic from the model being fitted; fitting a measured decay is what remains. Found on the way and fixed: `compute_av` *wrote* `simulation_grid_resolution` into `source_info` from `disc_step` rather than reading it, so both quenching benchmarks' `--resolution` flag was inert and every run — including PRD-110's — was at the 1.5 Å default while the validation pages said 2.5 Å (numbers unaffected, label corrected); it now raises on disagreement (`test/fret/test_av_resolution.py`). `benchmark/kq_sensitivity_analysis.py`, [`okf/validation/quenching_multisite.md`](../validation/quenching_multisite.md).

* ⛔ [PRD-110: a parametric physics-informed surrogate for the quenching field](prd-110.md) — **status: stopped at its stage-0 gate (2026-08-17); superseded by [PRD-111](prd-111.md).** PRD-109's field solver costs 2.26 s per 50 ns at `ng = 41` and scales as `dg⁻⁵` (the explicit limit `dt ≤ dg²/6D`), which is fine once and ruinous in the loop that matters: the PET rates ship as *"starting values meant to be calibrated against measured lifetimes"*, and a 9-parameter calibration wants O(10²–10³) solves **per site**. Scopes a network trained on the PDE residual and parametric in θ, so calibration becomes a gradient walk over a differentiable surrogate. Faces the crux rather than deferring it — **a PINN suits a fixed domain with varying parameters, and every labelling site is a different domain**, so the scope is per-site and geometry conditioning is a separate (neural-operator) PRD. Stage 0 deliberately writes **no network**: it measures whether θ is identifiable from a single decay at all, since `kQ` and `slow_factor` plausibly trade off, and a faster fit to a degenerate problem is not progress — **if it is not identifiable the PRD stops there.** Stage 0 deliberately wrote **no network** and measured identifiability first: **condition number 1.4 × 10¹⁰**, and a real fit reproduced the data while missing `kQ_scale` by −69 % and `free_diffusion` by −52 %. A faster fit to a degenerate problem is not progress, so the PRD stopped and its four open decisions — including where a DL dependency could live — were never needed. Findings that outlived it: the equilibrium occupancy has the closed form `p ∝ 1/D` (verified to 1.1e-13, and the iteration it replaced was not converging), `slow_factor` compounds per contacting atom so it is only meaningful near 1.0, the decay becomes exactly D-independent at small `slow_factor`, and gradients need a θ-independent time step. [`okf/validation/quenching_identifiability.md`](../validation/quenching_identifiability.md).
