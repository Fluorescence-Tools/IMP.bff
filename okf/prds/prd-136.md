## Why

A shipped rotamer library is *one fixed sample of the free dye*, reweighted
at a site and never resampled; at a tight site the effective sample size falls
to 1.7 conformers and moves E by 0.07
([`site_dependent_rotamer_sampling.md`](../validation/site_dependent_rotamer_sampling.md)).
That page's own conclusion — "the real fix is resampling, not reweighting" —
is what a site-conditioned generative model does. Three earlier attempts at a
learned dye model never learned from MD: PRD-110 stopped on identifiability,
PRD-135 learns the PDE solver, PRD-119 plans an MSM on the *free* dye. The
question the user brought back from a conference — Timewarp — is the missing
one: learn `p(x(t+Δt) | x(t))` from MD pairs at a large lag, transferably.

**Prior art (2026-09-07).** Nobody has published a learned, site-conditioned,
time-coarsened model of dye+linker dynamics. Closest: hand-built Langevin dye
models (Frost, Cook & Sanabria, *Phys Rev Res* 2025; spherical dyes, no
sticking, no site transfer), FRETpredict's free-dye libraries (2024), and the
2026 *JPCB* review (Sauve et al.) which lists no neural dye surrogate. The
machinery exists: Timewarp (Klein et al., NeurIPS 2023; flow proposal + MH,
2–4 aa peptides, no speedup beyond), ITO (NeurIPS 2023) and **TITO**
(Viguera Diez, Schreiner, Olsson, *Sci Adv* 2026, arXiv 2510.07589): PaiNN
velocity field, OT flow matching, conditioned on `x(t)` and the lag, trained
jointly on 12,530 small molecules (MDQM9-nc) and 1,457 tetrapeptides, no
energy at inference, Boltzmann marginals (JSD ≤ 0.35) and implied timescales
over three decades on unseen molecules, four orders of magnitude faster than
MD. UniSim (ICML 2025), FBM, MDGen, BioEmu, CGSchNet are the neighbours. The
user's own `avnn` (AV grid → 3D MAE → observable heads) did not work and is a
negative result.

## Decisions (tpeulen, 2026-09-07, by interview)

1. **State**: Cartesian, SE(3)-equivariant, TITO-faithful — not torsion space.
2. **Model**: `p(x_{t+Δt} | x_t, Δt, env)`, multi-lag training over
   {5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500 ps, 1, 2, 5 ns}; the environment is the
   protein heavy atoms within ~25 Å of the anchor as a point cloud with
   {element, residue class from `qpinn/voxel.py`, backbone/side-chain,
   distance to anchor}; free dye = empty environment, one model for both arms.
3. **No Metropolis correction in the product** (TITO's choice; no energy
   consistent with explicit-water training exists). Energies under the dry
   prmtop + GB serve as a validity diagnostic only.
4. **MD campaign, hybrid**: ~60 sites × 2 dyes as 20 Å fragments, backbone
   restrained, explicit TIP3P, 2 seeds × 25 ns (tranche 1, ≈ 25–30 GPU-days on
   heinzehub's two GPUs); the 22 measured Alexa488 T4L/hGBP1 sites as whole
   proteins, 200 ns (gold set, ≈ 340 GPU-h). Sites from `qpinn/sites.py`
   `exposed_sites`, stratified by neighbourhood residue class.
5. **Gates set by all three consumers**: PRD-119/135 (dipole `P2(t)`
   timescales, `r_inf`, bound fraction, exchange generator `L`; final check
   against the 23 measured sites where the continuum has `r_inf` r = +0.09
   out-of-sample), PRD-99 (site density vs the plain AV), PRD-116 (A48_C1R
   library parity, overlap ≥ the MD-replica ceiling 0.815).
6. **Dyes**: Alexa488-C1R plus Alexa647-C2R (AMBER-DYES carries both; the
   shipped `A64_C2R` library gives a parity reference; a free-dye A64_C2R run
   is added since only A64_B1R was simulated). The free-dye model trains on
   all 19 runs.
7. **Placement**: prototype in `prototypes/dye_timewarp/` (torch; exempt).
8. **pAcF linker (2026-09-07)**: the owner asked for the Alexa488–pAcF ketoxime label to be parameterised so the
   ten T4L `r_inf` sites join the campaign (ticket `T-20260907-05`). Resolved the same day without new work: AMBER-DYES
   `B1R` already *is* that label (rotamer-simulation's finding, confirmed here by substructure match on the A64_B1R
   topology), a shipped `AlexaFluor 488 B1R` library exists and places on 172L, but no `A48_B1R` production run exists (the converged B1R replicas are `AF4_B1R`, the Lumiprobe AF 488
   parameterisation with GAFF2/AM1-BCC charges — not interchangeable with AMBER-DYES `A48`). rotamer-simulation queues
   `A48_B1R` at 3 × 140 ns (~15 GPU-h) so arm A matches the parameters the site MD uses; the `A48_B1R` smoke run
   supplies the label topology meanwhile. The ten sites entered the campaign with linker `B1R` on 2026-09-07; sites 8,
   19 and 60 have every library conformer clashing under the screening (Z = 0) and start from the least-clash conformer.
   C++ consumers receive exported `.drot` ensembles + weights, `L` and lattice
   densities — never the flow. A C++ inference path is out of scope.

## Findings on the way

- **Gold set chemistry (2026-09-07).** Of the 15 measured Alexa488 T4L sites, ten (5, 8, 19, 22, 36, 44, 55, 60, 69, 70)
  carry the dye through a p-acetyl-phenylalanine hydroxylamine linkage — the ones with `r_inf` — and only five (86, 119,
  127, 132, 150; lifetimes and QY only) are cysteine-maleimide. All eight hGBP1 sites are cysteine-maleimide with `r_inf`.
  The C1R campaign's gold set is therefore 13 sites (`data/sites_gold.csv`), and the experimental `r_inf` check rests on
  hGBP1; the pAcF sites need their own linker parameters (a `dye_library` job) before they can be simulated.
- **Builder (`s06_build_site.py`).** A site's start conformer comes from FRETpredict's own site logic as shipped in
  `IMP.bff` (`RotamerEnsemble.from_site`, the cutoff10 library placed on the backbone frame and Boltzmann-screened —
  user's call, 2026-09-07, `s06a_place_rotamers.py`): replica 0 the top-weight rotamer, later replicas weight-
  proportional draws; the ensemble's Z and effective sample size are recorded per site (T4L 127: ESS 5.7, T4L 86: 115).
  A Kabsch graft of a free-dye frame remains the fallback when no seed exists; carving a 20 Å fragment
  with ACE/NME caps (the NME methyl is `C` in this AmberTools, not `CH3`), tleap with the N99–C99 bond and detected
  disulfides, OpenMM on OpenCL (heinzehub's driver cannot JIT the conda CUDA PTX) with 2 kcal/mol/Å² backbone restraints
  on fragments; T4L site 44 as a fragment is 60 residues / 14,161 atoms with water.

## Risks

- **Site generalisation** — the failure mode of `avnn` and of the early PINN
  (0.029 ns trained vs 0.257 ns held-out structures). Local equivariant
  conditioning, many short runs over many sites, and G8 as the go/no-go.
- **Force field, not nature** — AMBER-DYES/ff14SB sticking; only stage 6
  against measured `r_inf`/`ρ` says anything about reality.
- **Cross-dye transfer is thin at 17 labels** (TITO used 12,530 molecules);
  per-dye models are the fallback, Alexa488 and Alexa647 are what is paid for.
- **Compute** — heinzehub's two GPUs are shared with the PRD-116 dye-library lanes; cordeshub is saturated by the
  PRD-135 trainer. Owner's allocation (2026-09-07, arranged with rotamer-simulation): their `A48_B1R` × 3 runs
  first after the open-ring tier, then **GPU 0 is released to the `s07` queue automatically** (their queue exits on GPU 0 once the three A48_B1R logs are complete; then A64_C2R, the 56 site runs, two more A64_C2R replicas, and the queue writes `GPU0_RELEASED` to hand the card back) and
  their tier 4 continues on GPU 1 only; `free_v1` training shares GPU 1, the probe loop takes short bursts on GPU 0.
  Stages 0–3 deliver the library generator without any new MD.
