# PRD-141: one precise flat vocabulary for IMP.bff

**Status:** complete. The owner explicitly reaffirmed the full approved plan
on 2026-09-12, overriding the later reader-ticket hold. All families, splits,
internalization and direct-ptolib migration are implemented and verified.
[Completion evidence](../validation/taxonomy-completion.md) records full-suite,
header, non-unity and downstream checks and preserved pre-existing work.
The owner
approved the complete taxonomy map at `.omx/plans/prd-bff-file-taxonomy.md`.
This PRD is the durable implementation contract; the map remains the detailed
rename ledger until its entries land.

## Why

IMP.bff is an IMP module and therefore keeps the flat public header/source
layout used by `../imp/modules/*`. Its file list nevertheless contains names
that only describe history (`Node`, `Port`, `GreedyOlga`, `Labelizer*`, `Pto*`) or are
too broad to navigate (`Base`, `Bin`, `Compute`, `Scoring`). It also mixes
different domains behind the same word (`Rotamer` applies to probe conformers
and protein side chains) and lets a local PTO façade duplicate ptolib.

The solution is not directories. It is a precise, domain-first vocabulary that
sorts coherently in the flat layout, while preserving algorithms, numeric
results, serialised formats and dependencies.

## Decisions

- **Flat layout remains.** Public files remain in `include/*.h` and
  `src/*.cpp`; IMP-specific sources stay behind `src/ImpLayer.cpp`.
- **Graph / Fit / Inference / Bayesian / MCMC are separate.** The graph runtime
  owns laziness and ports; deterministic objectives own `Fit*`; structural
  factorisation owns `Inference*`; priors/posteriors/evidence own `Bayesian*`;
  sampling owns `MCMC*`.
- **Probe, not dye.** flrCIF vocabulary governs probe identity, data, force
  fields, accessible volumes, rotamers and molecular simulation. A position is
  intrinsic to a simulation/representation and is not repeated there.
- **Labelizer remains separate.** It scores how likely a structural position is
  to be labelled. Its historical abbreviations become full
  `Labelizer*` / `labelizer_*`; its command remains `imp_bff_labelizer`.
- **Pair selection is generic.** `ProbePairSelection*` takes predicted
  measurements and measurement error and is usable for FRET, EPR and PRE;
  actual transfer calculation remains `FRET*`.
- **FPS remains separate.** Its formats, project model, workflows and exports
  are the FPS product/compatibility boundary.
- **PTO is ptolib.** Bff removes `PtoReader`, `PtoWriter` and `PtoObject`, uses
  vendored `pto::File` / `pto::PtoObject` directly, and keeps only its MMFDB
  profile vocabulary.
- **Hard migration.** Bff is pre-release: no aliases or deprecation layer.

5. **Photophysics, measurement models, FPS and boundaries.** Land FRET,

   `MMFDBProfile`, migrate every known bff/sibling reference, and prove no old

   TU, complete header/export manifest and migrated downstream consumers.

## Done when

- Every approved map entry has one canonical filename and public spelling.
- No retired public name/header or compatibility alias remains.
- The full IMP and standalone builds, import tests, public API inventory and
  behavior/numerical regression suites pass.
- Known sibling consumers are migrated and searched clean.
- PTO container behavior is handled only by ptolib; bff owns no wrapper API.
- The user-facing docs and the resume point describe the completed taxonomy.

## Non-goals

- Nested public directories or departure from IMP module conventions.
- Algorithmic, numerical, performance, format or dependency changes.
- Altering existing PTO, MMFDB, FPS or reference-method provenance values.
- Aliases for retired public names.
