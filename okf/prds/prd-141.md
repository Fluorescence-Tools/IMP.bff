# PRD-141: one precise flat vocabulary for IMP.bff

**Status:** stages 1 and 2 complete; stage 3a probe simulation, accessible
volumes, template/force-field/topology/scoring/data files migrated on
2026-09-12. Both builds compile; no new regression failures. **Blocked next:**
probe/side-chain rotamer-library separation while T-20260910-01 owns the
unrelated reader changes. [Stage 3 evidence](../validation/taxonomy-stage3.md)
records the pre-existing potential-reader and molecular-setup failures.
The owner
approved the complete taxonomy map at `.omx/plans/prd-bff-file-taxonomy.md`.
This PRD is the durable implementation contract; the map remains the detailed
rename ledger until its entries land.

## Why

IMP.bff is an IMP module and therefore keeps the flat public header/source
layout used by `../imp/modules/*`. Its file list nevertheless contains names
that only describe history (`Node`, `Port`, `GreedyOlga`, `Labelizer*`, `Pto*`) or are
too broad to navigate (`Base`, `Bin`, `Compute`, `Scoring`). It also mixes
different domains behind the same word (`Rotamer` applies to probe conformers
and protein side chains) and lets a local PTO façade duplicate ptolib.

The solution is not directories. It is a precise, domain-first vocabulary that
sorts coherently in the flat layout, while preserving algorithms, numeric
results, serialised formats and dependencies.

## Decisions

- **Flat layout remains.** Public files remain in `include/*.h` and
  `src/*.cpp`; IMP-specific sources stay behind `src/ImpLayer.cpp`.
- **Graph / Fit / Inference / Bayesian / MCMC are separate.** The graph runtime
  owns laziness and ports; deterministic objectives own `Fit*`; structural
  factorisation owns `Inference*`; priors/posteriors/evidence own `Bayesian*`;
  sampling owns `MCMC*`.
- **Probe, not dye.** flrCIF vocabulary governs probe identity, data, force
  fields, accessible volumes, rotamers and molecular simulation. A position is
  intrinsic to a simulation/representation and is not repeated there.
- **Labelizer remains separate.** It scores how likely a structural position is
  to be labelled. Its historical abbreviations become full
  `Labelizer*` / `labelizer_*`; its command remains `imp_bff_labelizer`.
- **Pair selection is generic.** `ProbePairSelection*` takes predicted
  measurements and measurement error and is usable for FRET, EPR and PRE;
  actual transfer calculation remains `FRET*`.
- **FPS remains separate.** Its formats, project model, workflows and exports
  are the FPS product/compatibility boundary.
- **PTO is ptolib.** Bff removes `PtoReader`, `PtoWriter` and `PtoObject`, uses
  vendored `pto::File` / `pto::PtoObject` directly, and keeps only its MMFDB
  profile vocabulary.
- **Hard migration.** Bff is pre-release: no aliases or deprecation layer.

   existing behavior for every affected slice. No source rename until the

2. **Graph, Fit, Inference, Bayesian and MCMC.** Rename the core runtime and
   fitting/inference families, including ChiSurf callers and graph documents.
   **1a done:** `GraphNode`, `GraphPort`, `GraphSession`, `GraphEvaluation`,
   `GraphExpression`, `GraphObject`, `GraphLinkCycleError`, the graph port enum
   and helpers. Bff’s graph/public-API suite: 283 passed + 42 subtests;
   broader bff slice: 448 passed + 42 subtests; ChiSurf direct consumers:
   49 passed, 1 skipped + 69 subtests. **1b done:** `FitDataset`,
   `FitChiSquared`, `FitJointChiSquared`, `FitMinimizer`, the Fit enums,
   observer and residual helper. Bff: 496 passed, 5 skipped + 42 subtests;
   ChiSurf direct fitting boundary: 97 passed, 1 skipped + 69 subtests.
   **1c done:** `InferenceFactorGraph`, `InferenceFactorKind`,
   `InferenceJunctionTreeEdge` and `INFERENCE_FACTOR_*`; on-disk factor kind
   strings remain `PRIOR` / `LIKELIHOOD` / `HYPER`. Bff: 498 passed, 5 skipped
   + 42 subtests; ChiSurf factor-graph/sampler consumers: 55 passed.
   **1d done:** `MCMCSampler`, `MCMCSamplerConfigurationError`; ChiSurf
   `sampler_bff` and docking docs follow; no `Sampler` alias. Bff Graph/Fit/
   Inference/MCMC slice: 514 passed, 5 skipped + 42 subtests; ChiSurf
   factor-graph/sampler consumers: 55 passed. **1e done:** header-only
   `BayesianPSpline`, `BayesianTransforms`, `BayesianTransformedGaussianPrior`,
   `BayesianFisherScoring`, `BayesianLaplace`, and `BayesianDeltaMethod`.
   The regenerated all-header and explicit template/helper instantiations
   compile. **1f done:** `Optimization.h` and `OptimizationStepResult`, with
   a public all-header plus instantiated `DampedNewton` C++17 check.
3. **Labelizer and ProbePairSelection.** Split the merged Labelizer concepts
   into clear family files; replace historical abbreviations; generalize the
   selection vocabulary without changing its numeric kernel. **Implemented:**
   `LabelizerFeatures`, `LabelizerScore`, `LabelizerFRET`, `LabelizerIO`, and
   `ProbePairSelection` each have their own headers/sources. The AV provider
   belongs to FRET. `select_probe_pairs` / `select_probe_positions` accept
   `predicted_measurements` and `measurement_error`; ChiSurf forwards to the
   new API. Fixed FRET/EPR/PRE fixtures retain the prior pair/position order
   and precision. Labelizer implementation bodies and the selector algorithm
   are unchanged, verified by source comparison.
4. **Probe representations and simulation.** Rename the probe data/force-field
   family, accessible volume, molecular simulation, and probe rotamers. Split
   protein side-chain rotamers from probe rotamers. Do not touch
   `RotamerLibrary.*` until T-20260910-01 is resolved. **3a done:**
   `ProbeSimulation` / `ProbeSimulationTrajectory`, `MolecularProbeSimulation`,
   separate `ProbeDiffusionSimulation`, `ProbeAccessibleVolume*`, and the
   probe template/force-field/topology/potential/data file families.
   `RotamerScoring` owns the scoring orchestration. Missing direct includes
   exposed by the new ordering are fixed; standalone configuration removes
   obsolete generated public headers. **3b blocked:** probe/side-chain
   rotamers and their library cannot be separated while its reader work is
   still held by the other ticket.
5. **Photophysics, measurement models, FPS and boundaries.** Land FRET,
   Photophysics, FCS, TCSPC, FPS, IMP bridge, ComputeBackend, CommandLine,
   OpenMP, compatibility and internalization changes.
6. **PTO and final migration.** Remove the bff PTO façade, separate
   `MMFDBProfile`, migrate every known bff/sibling reference, and prove no old
   names remain.

## Done when

- Every approved map entry has one canonical filename and public spelling.
- No retired public name/header or compatibility alias remains.
- The full IMP and standalone builds, import tests, public API inventory and
  behavior/numerical regression suites pass.
- Known sibling consumers are migrated and searched clean.
- PTO container behavior is handled only by ptolib; bff owns no wrapper API.
- The user-facing docs and the resume point describe the completed taxonomy.

## Non-goals

- Nested public directories or departure from IMP module conventions.
- Algorithmic, numerical, performance, format or dependency changes.
- Altering existing PTO, MMFDB, FPS or reference-method provenance values.
- Aliases for retired public names.
