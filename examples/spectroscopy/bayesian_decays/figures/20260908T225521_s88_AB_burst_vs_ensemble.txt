A/B: the burst experiment against the prototype's own ensemble simulation.

Arm A is what `s88 --steps 9` does: the expected counts of the twelve PIE channels from one truth, the
partner pulses summed into eight histograms, one Poisson draw -- the data are exactly a realisation of the
model being fitted. Arm B is the bff example notebook: molecules drawn one at a time from the same p(R/R0),
each with its own Poisson photon budget, uncorrelated background over the whole record, scattered light, and
a tttrlib burst search deciding which photons are histogrammed. Everything else is identical, and each
sample's reference-channel counts and background fractions in arm A are set to the ones arm B produced.

5 seeds. (a) p(R/R0) of the first seed with the delta-method band, against the truth in the cuvette
and the truth behind the selected bursts. (b) deviance per degree of freedom against the reference measured
by Poisson draws at each fit's own means (grey). (c) runs tests of every histogram of every seed. (d) pulls
per summary, bars the mean pull. (e) the ratio of posterior widths, B over A -- what the experiment costs.
